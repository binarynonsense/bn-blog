<ul><li>Added a new tool: Template Maker. A direct port of my comic book page and thumbnails maker app.
</li><li>Added a new option in the preferences to set the clock format (12h or 24h).
</li><li>Added a "Close File" entry to the right-click context menu.
</li><li>Added a new entry in the menu (Help &gt; Check for Updates) to check  if there's a newer stable version available on github and give the  option to open its download page in the browser if there's one.
</li><li>Added Filipino localization (contributed by <a href="https://github.com/CodeByMoriarty">AndrewL (CodeByMoriarty)</a>).
</li><li>Disabled forced colors when using a high contrast OS theme.
</li><li>Modified the color themes.
</li><li>Fixed dragging and dropping files no longer working and producing errors and crashes due to a breaking change in Electron's API.
</li></ul>
